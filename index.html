A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious?
We like that.

Here’s how the offline part really works.

Does PocketPass give me internet without a connection?+

No. It stores selected content on your device while you have Wi-Fi. That content is available offline later; live websites and messaging still need a connection.

What kind of content can I save?+

That depends on the content providers connected to the service. A real launch needs agreements and integrations for each source.

How does payment and access work?+

After a payment provider confirms your purchase on the server, your account can access its subscription. This demo shows the flow but does not process payments.

Can this work with a Wi-Fi router?+

Yes. A compatible router can show a captive portal sign-in page and grant Wi-Fi access after authentication. It still needs a secure website and server to manage accounts, payments, and network access.
